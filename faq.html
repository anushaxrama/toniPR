FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Quick answers before you start. Contact support anytime.

Is ToniPR only for startups?

No. ToniPR is built for anyone who needs consistent, professional content from their expertise—small business owners, independent consultants, advisors, professional services firms, and teams—not only venture-backed founders. If you want to sound credible online and keep channels active without living in a doc all week, you’re the kind of customer we had in mind.

Who is ToniPR for?

Founders, consultants, coaches, agencies, and local or online businesses that rely on trust and clarity. Typical use cases: LinkedIn and social posts, articles, short video clips, press-ready quotes, and boilerplate you can reuse on your site or in proposals.

Do I need a PR or marketing background?

No. You show up for a guided conversation (or type in chat). Toni structures questions around your business and goals; you answer in plain language. The outputs are formatted so you can copy, paste, and publish.

What do I get after one conversation?

A full content kit aligned to the goal you set—things like posts for LinkedIn and X, narrative and article drafts, short clips, quote banks, and supporting copy. Exact items can vary by plan; see Pricing for session details.
