Pricing FAQ

What’s the difference between Starter and Founder?

Starter is a one-time purchase for one session at the current launch price ($39). Founder is $299 per year for 12 sessions you can use anytime. Both include the same full content bundle each time—no watered-down version on Starter.

What counts as one session, and what’s included in the price?

One session is one AI interview with Toni (about 5–10 minutes, no prep). Each session includes the full kit: social posts, articles, press-style quotes, your story, and the rest of the bundle shown above. There are no add-ons or per-item fees—the price is for the whole package.

Do unused Founder sessions expire? What does “roll over” mean?

On the Founder plan, sessions you don’t use in a year carry forward—they roll over so you don’t lose what you paid for. Starter is a single session, so there’s nothing to roll over.

Does the Starter promo or discount apply to the Founder plan?

No. Launch and promotional pricing apply to Starter only. The annual Founder plan is already priced as the best value per session (about $25 each vs. the list price).

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if ToniPR isn’t a fit—same terms you’ll see at checkout.
