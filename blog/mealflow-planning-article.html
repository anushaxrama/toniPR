Generated article

A family of five was spending hours each week on dinner decisions. After trying MealFlow, they cut meal planning to under 2 minutes. Here's how they did it.

The problem

Sarah and Mike have three kids under 10. Between work, school pickups, and activities, the question "what's for dinner?" became a daily source of stress. They'd spend 20 to 30 minutes most nights just deciding, then another 15 minutes checking what they had in the fridge.

"That's not just time saved. That's less stress at the dinner table."

What changed

MealFlow gave them weekly recipes based on their preferences, dietary needs, and what was already in their pantry. No more scrolling through recipes. No more "I don't know, what do you want?" The app suggested a plan in under 2 minutes.

The result

Three hours a week became two minutes. The family now has a rotating menu they actually enjoy, and grocery lists that match. Dinner went from a daily negotiation to something that just happens.
