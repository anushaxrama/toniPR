Content for Consultants: Credibility Without Living in Google Docs

April 2026 · 6 min read

Your buyers check LinkedIn before they book a call. They skim your site, your last few posts, and whether you sound like a real expert—or like everyone else. For consultants and advisors, credible content isn’t vanity; it’s proof you think clearly and stay current.

Why consultants stall on content

Most independent practitioners aren’t short on ideas. They’re short on time and a repeatable format. Client work wins every week. The empty doc for “this month’s LinkedIn post” loses.

What actually builds trust

  • Specific stories from your work (anonymized when needed)—not generic tips everyone repeats.
  • One clear point of view on the problems you see in your niche.
  • Consistency—showing up often enough that people remember you when the need arises.

LinkedIn and beyond

LinkedIn is still the default for B2B and professional services, but the same core narrative can feed articles, email introductions, podcast pitches, and short clips. The goal is one coherent story, adapted per channel—not starting from scratch every time.
